Courses / Basic Scooty Training

Basic Scooty Training

15-class beginner-first training program.

Who is it for?

Especially useful for complete beginners and people who cannot ride a bicycle.

15-Class Progression

  1. Balance and posture
  2. Brake and accelerator control
  3. Starting and stopping
  4. Turning practice
  5. Trainer-guided road practice
  6. Traffic awareness
  7. Helmet and safety practice
  8. Independent riding confidence

Class Format

Approximately 30 minutes per class. Flexible trainer-led scheduling.

Courses / Bike Training

Bike Training

Learn clutch, gear, acceleration and coordination step-by-step.

What you learn

  • Clutch bite point and smooth release
  • Gear shifting
  • Acceleration coordination
  • Starting and stopping
  • Turning and slow-speed control
  • Road and traffic handling
  • Practical RTO preparation where applicable

The fundamentals of clutch, gear, acceleration and coordination are common across motorcycles. Higher-displacement riding should always be approached progressively and responsibly.

Frequently Asked Questions

বাংলা + English answers for common questions.

আমি সাইকেল চালাতে পারি না — স্কুটি শিখতে পারবো?

হ্যাঁ। Training basic balance, control and coordination থেকে step-by-step শুরু করা যায়।

How long is Basic Scooty Training?

The basic program is structured as 15 classes, approximately 30 minutes per class.

Do you offer doorstep training?

Yes. Doorstep training is available up to 10 KM from Netaji Metro, Kudghat, subject to availability.

What is the doorstep fee?

0–2 KM: ₹4,500. 2–3 KM: ₹5,000. 3–10 KM: distance-based fee up to ₹8,000.

How can I book?

Use the booking form, call or WhatsApp. The production backend can connect bookings to Google Sheets, Apps Script and admin notifications.
